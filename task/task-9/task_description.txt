task_description: |
  Users keep missing important stuff because there's no email notifications. Someone assigns them 
  a task and they don't know unless they log in. Need to fix this.
  
  Email types needed:
  - Task assignment ("You've been assigned to X")
  - @mentions in comments ("John mentioned you in a comment")
  - Due date reminders (24hr before and 1hr before)
  - Workspace invites
  
  Use Bull or similar for background jobs - don't want email sending to slow down the API. Queue 
  them up and send async.
  
  Email templates need to look decent - HTML version for nice clients and plain text fallback. 
  Include unsubscribe links (legally required in some places!).
  
  User preferences page - let people choose which emails they want. Some people want everything, 
  some just want @mentions. Their choice.
  
  Scheduled reminders are tricky - need a job that checks for upcoming due dates and sends emails. 
  Run every hour maybe?
  
  Unsubscribe needs to actually work - use tokens in the URL so people can't unsubscribe others. 
  Update their preferences when they click it.
  
  Handle failures gracefully - retry failed emails with exponential backoff. Don't spam people if 
  the email server is down.
